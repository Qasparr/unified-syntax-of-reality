## III. ANALYSIS — THE SCRIBE'S THOUGHTS THROUGHOUT THE AUDIT

**On the Simplicio line (the one correction).** The v2 draft said Urban
was "widely believed to have been caricatured" as Simplicio. The sources
attest something more precise and more interesting: *Urban himself*
believed it — "Urban even suspected that Simplicius was intended as a
caricature of his own person," and the perception, true or not, is what
made him implacable. "Widely believed" blurred who believed what. The
line now reads: "a former friend who took the *Dialogue*'s fool,
Simplicio, as a caricature of his own person and never pardoned the
insult." The gate prefers the sharper true thing to the vaguer true-ish
thing.

**On the Vatican Observatory nuance (deliberately excluded).** The
Observatory's piece on the 1616 verdict traces four layers of
translation — Latin original, Italian paraphrase, Riccioli's Latin,
English — and a disputed semicolon. It is excellent scholarship and it
changes nothing in the thesis: the standard English rendering
("foolish and absurd in philosophy") is what every general history uses,
and the thesis uses it as REPORTED standard history, not as a philological
claim. Adding the semicolon would be precision theater. Excluded on
purpose; logged here so the exclusion is visible.

**On "the father contributes none" (boundary, not a change).** The
literature documents rare exceptions — a 2018 PNAS paper found 17 cases
of biparental mtDNA inheritance across three family trees. The rule
stands as the rule: maternal inheritance is the bedrock principle, and
the thesis states the rule, not the absence of exceptions. No revision
required; the boundary is logged so no one mistakes the rule for an
absolute.

**On the v1-carried entries.** Re-pulling Einstein and Sagan from the
live web would have produced the same verdicts at the cost of ritual. The
v1 audit is the evidence; the gate re-confirmed the record rather than
re-performing it. This is stated so the audit's coverage is honest about
its seams.

**On the author's material.** The Genesis code, the Many == Elohim
equation, FAFO, the flagpole, TRVVTH — all correctly flagged as his
throughout. The gate found no instance of his dictation being presented
as external authority, and no external authority being bent to serve his
